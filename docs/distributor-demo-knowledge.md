Version: demo-2026-10-19-v2. All company names identify fictional test scenarios. These are not actual company policies, prices, coverage or quotations. Each fact repeats its distributor name so a 300-token chunk remains understandable. A business day excludes Sundays and holidays. Prices exclude tax; compare identical weights, routes and service conditions. Live tracking, real contacts, discounts, fuel surcharges, insurance and volumetric-weight rules are not documented.

- BlueDart pickup cutoff: 14:00 local time, Monday-Saturday excluding holidays. BlueDart maximum parcel weight: 20 kg.
- BlueDart COD order limit: INR 20,000. BlueDart COD fee: INR 40 or 2% of collected amount, whichever is higher. BlueDart COD settlement: within 3 business days after successful delivery.
- BlueDart delivery attempts: 2; then return to origin.
- BlueDart reverse pickup: merchant approval required; request within 7 calendar days after delivery.
- BlueDart delays: open a merchant-support ticket with airway-bill number and pickup date; escalate after 24 hours unresolved.
- BlueDart tracking: airway-bill number; Created, Picked Up, In Transit, Out for Delivery, Delivered.
